What still sounded generated

  • Three paragraphs used the same negative contrast pattern: "not X, but Y."
  • The rhythm was too tidy, with each section ending in a prepared lesson.
  • "Final piece of data" sounded like a manufactured closer.
  • The strongest facts, including 2.50 GPa, 248.68 g, and 16.000 kg, arrived later than the general claims.

Final rewrite used in the site

Our team began by measuring how pasta broke and bent. We converted those results into conservative material inputs, screened several truss families, and kept revising around mass, clearance, buckling, and what we could actually build.

The bridge started with broken pasta. Tension, bending, and buckling tests came before member sizing.

Bending returned 2.85 GPa, while buckling returned 7.55 GPa under the assumed end factor. We treated that spread as uncertainty and designed with 2.50 GPa.

Mass and clearance decided which load path we could build. Iteration 05 traded 1.56 kg of modeled capacity for a 248.68 g design with enough vehicle clearance.

The physical bridge held 16.000 kg against the model's 50 kg design target and placed 6th of 72 teams. The result measured what the axial model could not: joint behavior, lateral restraint, fabrication damage, and load introduction.
